About VISUTRA

A home utility and handloom products brand by Visu Trader.

VISUTRA is a home-utility brand operated by Visu Trader. We focus on practical home furnishing and appliance-cover products for everyday use. Our current range includes washing machine covers, fridge top covers, TV covers and table covers.

We serve customers primarily through e-commerce marketplaces — Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho — and welcome enquiries from retailers, resellers and bulk buyers directly.

What we make

Each category is built around one job: keep dust, spills and everyday wear off an appliance or surface, without getting in the way of using it. Covers include practical details like storage pockets on the fridge top cover and a cable cutout on the TV cover, sized for common appliance dimensions.
