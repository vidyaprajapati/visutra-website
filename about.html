About VISUTRA

A home utility and handloom products brand by Visu Trader.

VISUTRA is a home-utility brand operated by Visu Trader. We focus on practical home furnishing and appliance-cover products for everyday use. Our current range includes washing machine covers, fridge top covers, TV covers and table covers.

We serve customers primarily through e-commerce marketplaces — Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho — and welcome enquiries from retailers, resellers and bulk buyers directly.

Why we make appliance covers

Most Indian homes keep their washing machine, fridge, or TV in a spot exposed to dust, cooking splatter, or direct sunlight through a window or balcony door. Over time, that takes a visible toll — faded plastic, dust-clogged buttons, a fridge top that never quite looks clean no matter how often it's wiped down. A well-fitted cover solves this with almost no daily effort: put it on, take it off to use the appliance, put it back. We built VISUTRA around that one simple job, done well, rather than trying to be a general home-decor brand.

What we make

Each category is built around one job: keep dust, spills and everyday wear off an appliance or surface, without getting in the way of using it. Covers include practical details like storage pockets on the fridge top cover and a cable cutout on the TV cover, sized for common appliance dimensions.

  • Washing machine covers — sized for both compact (6–6.5 kg) and standard (7–8.5 kg) fully-automatic machines, in waterproof, dust-proof fabric that holds up to daily use on a balcony or utility area.
  • Fridge top covers — sized to the flat top surface of standard refrigerators, with built-in storage pockets that turn otherwise wasted space into somewhere useful.
  • TV covers — sized by screen inches, with a cable cutout so you're not removing the whole cover just to reach a remote sensor or HDMI port.
  • Table covers — everyday wipeable protection for dining and utility tables.

If you're unsure which size fits your appliance, our Buying Guide walks through how to measure correctly for each category, and our team is reachable on WhatsApp for a quick check before you order.

How we think about quality

We size our products against real, common appliance dimensions rather than a single generic size — which is why you'll see specific capacity and screen-size bands across our listings instead of one "universal fit" claim that rarely actually fits well. Fabric choice is waterproof and dust-proof by default, since a cover that can't handle a splash or a monsoon-damp balcony isn't doing its job. We'd rather a customer measure carefully and get the right size the first time than deal with a returned cover that never fit.
