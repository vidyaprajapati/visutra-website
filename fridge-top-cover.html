FRIDGE TOP COVER GUIDE

Fridge top cover: size & buying guide

How to measure the top surface of a refrigerator and choose a useful everyday cover.

The top of a refrigerator often becomes a dust-collecting surface and an informal storage area. A fridge top cover can make that surface easier to maintain, but the cover should be selected from the refrigerator's actual top dimensions rather than its litre capacity alone.

Measure the top surface

Measure the width from side to side and the depth from front to back across the flat top surface. Note whether the top has a curved edge, raised trim, display panel or other obstruction. Compare your measurements with the cover dimensions before ordering.

How much larger should it be?

A little extra fabric can provide a useful edge overhang and make placement easier. The correct amount depends on the cover design. Avoid choosing a size so large that fabric hangs down the refrigerator sides unnecessarily or interferes with doors and handles.

Using storage pockets

The VISUTRA fridge top cover includes storage pockets. Use them for lightweight, everyday items such as kitchen cloths or small accessories. Do not overload pockets or store heavy objects where they could make the cover shift or put unnecessary stress on its stitching.

Cleaning routine

Because refrigerators are commonly located in kitchens, the cover may collect fine dust and occasional food-related splashes. Wipe it regularly. For deeper cleaning, follow the product care instructions and allow the fabric to dry fully before putting it back on the refrigerator.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing by refrigerator litre capacity without measuring the top.
  • Ignoring the depth of the top surface.
  • Buying a cover so small that it sits tightly on the surface.
  • Putting heavy objects into storage pockets.
  • Covering vents or other parts that should remain unobstructed.
