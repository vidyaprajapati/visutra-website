Orders From Buyers

Accepting an order fetches the current price, GST rate and HSN from your own Product Master (never anything the buyer sent), checks stock, then generates a GST invoice, deducts inventory, and logs a stock movement — all in one atomic step, so a double-click or two staff members clicking Accept at the same moment can never double-process the same order.
