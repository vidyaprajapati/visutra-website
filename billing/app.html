Mark This Period as Filed

Once you've actually submitted this return on the GST portal, mark it filed here — every invoice and purchase in this period then becomes locked from editing or deletion, so the records behind an already-submitted return can't drift out of sync with what you filed. This can't be undone from the app; contact yourself-as-support (i.e. edit Firestore directly) if you need to unlock a period marked by mistake.
