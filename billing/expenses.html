Business expenses

Things you buy to run the business, not to resell — packing material, label rolls, courier charges, tape, ads and so on. They are taken off your gross profit, so Profit & Graphs (and the Buyer Stock → Monthly Profit) show a true net profit / loss every month. Amounts are worked out ex-GST; tick "I claim this GST" if you take it back as input credit (then it is not a cost).
