Monthly Inventory Reconciliation

If you resell what you buy from sellers on your own Meesho/Flipkart shop, upload the report(s) you download for the month here to keep this stock accurate. Everything is read in your browser only. Meesho and Flipkart reports are recognised automatically by their columns — delivered items reduce stock, and cancelled/RTO/returned items add stock back, since those physically come back to you. Other formats fall back to treating every row as a sale. Each row is matched to one of your Buyer SKU Master products above (not the Seller/Billing side's Products) — the first time you upload, you'll map each SKU to one of them once, remembered automatically for future months.
