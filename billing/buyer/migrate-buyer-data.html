One-Time: Move Your Buyer Data to Its Own Storage

Buyer and Seller used to share the same Suppliers/Purchases/Payments records under one account — which is why Seller-side purchase data was showing up in the Buyer Dashboard, Purchase Entry, and Payment Entry. That's now fixed going forward: the Buyer side has its own separate collections. This one-time tool moves whatever you already entered through the Buyer side into that new, separate storage, so it doesn't disappear from view.

Safe to run more than once — anything already moved is skipped automatically. Run it once now, then you shouldn't need it again.

Check Any That Are Actually Buyer Suppliers

These suppliers have no way to auto-detect which side they belong to (only ones linked to an actual VISUTRA seller account can be told apart automatically). If a name below is someone you buy from as a Buyer (a local shop, a one-off supplier), check it. Leave unchecked anything that's really a material/supply source for your own Seller business — those stay exactly where they are.
