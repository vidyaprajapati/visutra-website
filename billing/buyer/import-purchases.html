Import Historical Purchase Data

Bring your old day-wise purchase records in from Excel/CSV — supplier dues, GSTR-1 Filing, and Purchase Entry's history will all reflect them, exactly as if you'd typed each one in by hand. Each row is matched to one of your products in Buyer SKU Master — by SKU first (your own SKU, the seller's SKU, or an Amazon/Meesho/Flipkart SKU), then by product name — and matched quantities are added to that product's Stock, same as any other purchase. Before importing you can check every match and pick the right product for anything unmatched; your picks are remembered for the next import.

New to this? Download the blank template, fill it in, then upload it below — its headers are already exactly what this page expects, so every column maps itself automatically.
