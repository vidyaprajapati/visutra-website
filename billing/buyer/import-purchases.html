Import Historical Purchase Data

Bring your old day-wise purchase records in from Excel/CSV — supplier dues, GSTR-1 Filing, and Purchase Entry's history will all reflect them, exactly as if you'd typed each one in by hand. A supplier name that doesn't already exist in your My Sellers off-platform list is created automatically.

This does not change your current Stock numbers. These are old purchases you've already used or resold since — adding their quantities to today's stock would double-count. If you also want the stock side of this period recorded, use Stock's own Monthly Inventory Reconciliation upload for the same months separately, or adjust the current stock number there by hand once you know what's actually on the shelf.

Step 1 — Match Your Columns

Date and Supplier are required. Everything else is optional — leave as "Not in my sheet" for anything you don't have, and a sensible default is used instead.
