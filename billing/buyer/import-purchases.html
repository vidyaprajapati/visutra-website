Import Historical Purchase Data

Bring your old day-wise purchase records in from Excel/CSV — supplier dues, GSTR-1 Filing, and Purchase Entry's history will all reflect them, exactly as if you'd typed each one in by hand. A Product Name that matches one of your Buyer SKU Master products (by name) also adds that quantity to its Stock — same as any other purchase. A name with no match still imports fine, just without a stock effect; the preview below marks which rows that applies to.

New to this? Download the blank template, fill it in, then upload it below — its headers are already exactly what this page expects, so every column maps itself automatically.

Step 1 — Match Your Columns
