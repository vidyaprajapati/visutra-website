Upload Shipping Labels

Upload one or more Amazon, Meesho, or Flipkart shipping-label PDFs (the kind you download to print and stick on the parcel — the label and/or tax invoice in the same file). Each page is scanned for a marketplace SKU. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — parsing happens entirely in your browser, same as the free Label Cropper tool. Every unit found here is deducted from that product's Stock right away (it's being dispatched), and the order below only asks the seller for whatever's short after your own stock covers as much of it as possible — a zero-inventory target, not a top-up.
