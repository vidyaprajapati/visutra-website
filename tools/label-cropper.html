Who's printing?
Drop PDF here or click to browse — Flipkart / Meesho / Amazon label PDF

Saved as your default.

Amazon's bulk PDF puts each shipping label on an odd page and its matching tax invoice on the next (even) page. With this on, only the label pages are kept in the output, and the SKU (the text in parentheses before "HSN:") plus the Qty from that line item's Qty column are read from the invoice and printed at the bottom of that label — e.g. "SKU: led-clr-55 x2". Orders with more than one product on the invoice print every SKU/Qty pair.

Turn on both to sort by courier partner first, then by SKU within each courier — handy for packing SKU-by-SKU within each courier's run. The SKU is read from the "Product Details" block printed on every Meesho label.

Meesho prints the SKU in very small text in the Product Details block. With this on, the same SKU and Qty are read off that block and reprinted in bigger, clearer text at the bottom of the cropped label — e.g. "SKU: VST-WFAT-7-8.5-FA32 x1".

Flipkart prints the SKU in a small "SKU ID | Description" table on the label (and again on the tax invoice, next to "IMEI/SrNo"). With this on, that SKU and Qty are read off and reprinted in bigger, clearer text at the bottom of the cropped label — e.g. "SKU: VST-WA-FA30-6_7.5 x1".
