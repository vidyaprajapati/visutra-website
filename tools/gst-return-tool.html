    Your GSTR-1 ready file

    An Excel workbook with B2B, B2CL, B2CS and HSN sheets, formatted to match the GST offline tool template — plus a full source-data sheet for your records.

    Before you file: this tool calculates totals from the reports you upload — it doesn't file anything on your behalf. Import the downloaded Excel into the GST portal's Returns Offline Tool to generate the upload JSON, and reconcile the totals against your own books first. GST rates, thresholds and formats change from time to time — verify the current B2CL threshold and tax rates on the GST portal before relying on this output.
