How sellers use these together

01

Design your product labels

Use Label Studio to make price tags and QR labels for your own products — export a batch straight from your SKU list.

02

Download your dispatch PDF

Get the shipping-label PDF from Flipkart, Meesho or Amazon Seller Central as usual — no change to your normal workflow.

03

Crop and print

Run it through Label Cropper to strip the invoice and whitespace, then send the clean label straight to your thermal printer.

Why it's free

These are internal tools we built for our own VISUTRA dispatch process and are sharing as-is. No account, no watermark, no upsell — just what we use ourselves.

Where your data goes

Nothing is uploaded to a server. Both tools run entirely in your browser tab — close it and everything is gone, including any PDF you crop.
