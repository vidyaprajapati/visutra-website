Appliance cover guides & resources

Practical information for choosing, using and caring for appliance and home covers.

Buying an appliance cover is not only about choosing a colour or pattern. The useful questions are: will it fit, what parts should it cover, how should it be cleaned, and when should it be removed? This resource center collects the practical information we use when explaining VISUTRA products to customers.

Care and maintenance

Fabric covers generally last longer when dust is removed regularly, stains are cleaned without harsh chemicals, and wet covers are dried completely before storage. During humid or monsoon weather, avoid folding a damp cover into a closed cupboard.

Questions before you order?

If you cannot decide between two sizes, send VISUTRA the appliance brand, model and your measurements through the enquiry form or WhatsApp. We recommend measuring the physical appliance rather than guessing from capacity or screen size alone.
